# Enclosure (3D print, two parts)

69.5 × 52.8 × 16.8 mm closed. Generated by `gen_enclosure.py` (CadQuery) from the board's 3D model, so every opening is placed from the real part geometry.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `enclosure_base.stl` | Base, in print orientation (flat underside on the bed) |
| `enclosure_lid.stl` | Lid, already flipped for printing (top face on the bed) |
| `enclosure_*.step` | Editable CAD; `enclosure_assembly.step` has both parts in place |
| `../docs/enclosure/*.png` | Renders with and without the board |

## Printing
- PETG or PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 20 % infill. No supports: every opening is open towards the build plate's opposite side or bridges less than 5 mm.
- The lid prints top-down, so the engraved labels are on the first layer. The two flex buttons and their plungers print in place.

## Assembly
1. Fit the board into the base. The SMA threads drop into the half-holes in the right wall, and the board rests on two screw standoffs and four support posts.
2. Put the lid on. The tongue on the base wall locates it, and the two lid pillars land on the board's mounting holes.
3. Fit 2 × M2 × 12 mm screws (pan or countersunk head) from underneath. Each goes through the base, the board (H1, H2) and into the 1.7 mm pilot hole in the lid pillar. They self-tap into the plastic, so don't over-tighten. M2 heat-set inserts (Ø3.2) also work if the pilot holes are drilled out.
4. Optional: SMA nuts and washers on the outside lock the connectors to the wall.

## Openings
- **USB-C:** sized 13.4 × 7.4 mm, so most cable overmolds reach the receptacle.
- **SMA:** 6.5 mm holes on the split line. The square SMA flange bears on the inside of the wall, so the wall takes the antenna's tightening and bending loads instead of the solder joints.
- **Screw terminals:** open from the top and the front, for screwdriver and wire access.
- **GPIO header:** a window in the lid.
- **USR/PWR LEDs:** 2.2 mm holes.
- **RST/BOOT:** flexure tabs in the lid with plungers. The plungers stop 0.3 mm above the switch.

## Checks done
- Each part is a single solid, and the base and lid don't overlap.
- There is zero interference with any board component in the 3D model, including the SMA flange (0.1 mm from the wall) and the button plungers.

## Regenerate
```
python3.11 gen_enclosure.py                       # STL/STEP
kicad-cli pcb export glb --subst-models -o build/board.glb ../lr2021_esp32s3.kicad_pcb
xvfb-run -a python render.py                      # needs pyvista
```
